# Use Power Apps on mobile devices

Power Apps offers two ways to deliver apps to mobile users:

| Experience | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Power Apps mobile player** | Running canvas and model-driven apps in the Power Apps app for iOS, Android, or Windows. |
| **Power Apps native apps** | Building standalone, branded iOS and Android apps with React Native, AI-guided authoring, and device capabilities. |

## Power Apps mobile player

Use the Power Apps mobile player when your users need to run canvas or model-driven apps from one installed app. The player supports capabilities such as offline use, push notifications, deep links, and mobile device optimization.

To get started, see [Install the Power Apps mobile app](run-powerapps-on-mobile.md).

## Power Apps native apps

Use Power Apps native apps when you need a standalone app, custom branding, native performance, or deeper access to device capabilities. Native apps connect to Dataverse, Microsoft 365, and Power Platform connectors and are deployed through Wrap.

To learn more, see [Power Apps native apps overview (preview)](native-apps/overview.md).
